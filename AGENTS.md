- Keep paid services disabled unless the owner explicitly approves them. MongoDB Atlas Free is now explicitly approved; other original stack constraints still apply.
- Distinguish verified functionality from provider-dependent or unfinished features. Never replace unavailable intelligence with fabricated successful reports.
