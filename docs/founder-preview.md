# Founder preview

After following the [setup and demo instructions](setup.md), open http://localhost:5180/#/home and select **Genesis Demo — Synthetic**. All company and market examples in this workspace are fictional.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. **Founder brief:** see attention signals, cash runway, connected customer ARR, and upcoming commitments.
2. **Risks & opportunities:** open evidence, then assign an action. Search and pagination keep the list compact.
3. **People & delivery:** compare Engineering pressure with available Product & Growth capacity.
4. **Customer health:** inspect Acme's declining usage and Bright Health's expansion signal. Move through 12 accounts with pagination.
5. **Runway & scenarios:** choose a saved scenario, compare up to three, and move the month slider to inspect cash. New scenarios preserve the actual baseline.
6. **Action board:** select Completed to inspect the billing recovery example.
7. **Decision journal:** inspect the reviewed billing decision and its lesson; record a new decision when ready.
8. **Company map:** follow a project to features, customers, and connected revenue.
9. **Market watch / Ask Genesis:** inspect labeled fictional market observations and answers drawn from saved evidence.
10. **Idea workspace:** the original startup analysis remains available on a separate page. No fabricated completed analysis was inserted.

The sidebar groups all 13 views by founder task. Hash links support browser back/forward and direct navigation. Workspace choice persists locally. Customer, priority, action, decision, question, market-history, and company-record lists paginate. Longer evidence is collapsed until requested. Mobile navigation opens from Menu; reduced-motion preferences disable decorative motion.

## Demo inventory after enrichment

4 teams, 8 employees, 6 projects, 12 customers, 6 actions, 4 decisions, 6 scenarios, 4 company observations, 7 financial snapshots, 2 fictional market observations, and 8 saved questions. New observations preserve previous baselines. The original non-demo workspace is unchanged.
